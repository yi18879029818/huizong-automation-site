## Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through the coolyne website, including contact forms, direct inquiry submissions, and communications initiated through the site.

## Information we collect

We may collect information you voluntarily submit, including:

- Full name
- Company name
- Email address
- Phone number or WhatsApp number
- Project scope, operational requirements, and other inquiry details

We may also collect standard website usage information needed for analytics, security, and site performance monitoring.

## How we use information

We may use collected information to:

- Respond to consultation requests and sales inquiries
- Evaluate automation project requirements
- Provide product, solution, and implementation information
- Improve website performance, lead routing, and communication workflows
- Maintain lawful internal business records

## Data sharing

We do not sell personal information submitted through this website. Information may be shared with internal personnel, service providers, or technical partners only where reasonably necessary to respond to an inquiry, evaluate a project, support website operations, or comply with law.

## Cookies and analytics

This website may use analytics and similar technologies to measure website performance, understand traffic patterns, and improve user experience. These tools may collect standard browser, device, and page interaction data.

## Data retention

We may retain submitted information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the inquiry, maintain business records, manage ongoing discussions, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

## Your choices

You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information you have submitted, subject to legal, contractual, and operational requirements.

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or about the handling of your information, please contact coolyne through our website contact page.
